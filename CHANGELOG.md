## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-23

### Added

- Menu-bar app with Accessibility onboarding, enable/pause toggle and launch at login.
- Smooth scrolling for mouse wheels (smoothness, speed, reverse direction, flick acceleration).
- Scroll modifiers: horizontal (⇧), precise (⌥), fast (⌃).
- Button actions on click / double / triple click / hold for buttons 3–8: Mission Control,
  Application Windows, Show Desktop, Launchpad, Spaces, Back/Forward, Middle Click,
  Look Up, Zoom, and recorded keyboard shortcuts.
- Hold-and-drag gestures (Spaces & Mission Control, grab-and-scroll) and hold-and-scroll
  actions (zoom, horizontal scroll, Spaces).
- Homebrew cask via `andresousadotpt/homebrew-tap`.
